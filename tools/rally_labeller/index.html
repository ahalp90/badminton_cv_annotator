Keys

        

        
Marks

        
    
          
  • ▲ Top / ▼ Bottom: a contact by the far / near player. Rallies are worked out from contacts.
    • 
          
  • ‖ Break: ends the rally at its last contact; the next contact starts a new rally.
    • 
          
  • ⊢ Rally start / ⊣ Rally end: optional exact boundaries.
    • 
          
  • [ Section start / ] Section end: the stretch you have fully labelled. Frames outside every section count as unlabelled.
    • 
          
  • Tags: no effect means the mark changes nothing (an end or break with no rally open, or an unpaired section mark). outside sections means the mark sits where nothing counts as labelled yet.
    • 
        

        
Mouse and touch

        
    
          
  • Click the video to play or pause.
    • 
          
  • The upper strip shows a few seconds either side of the playhead; the lower strip shows the whole part. Click either to go there. Click a mark on the upper strip, or in the list, to go to it.
    • 
          
  • Wrong side: go to the contact and press the other side's key. Wrong frame: delete it (X) and mark it again.
    • 
          
  • On a phone: double-tap the upper or lower half of the picture for a top or bottom contact; taps right on the middle don't count. Drag sideways on the video to step through frames.
