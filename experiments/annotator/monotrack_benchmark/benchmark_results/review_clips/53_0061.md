The court-fitting frame is about **12.6 seconds into this clip**, during the brief side-on view showing less than a quarter of the court. Both per-rally detectors produce distorted courts there. That one fit is then used across the rally, even though most of the footage shows the normal view.

MonoTrack crashes while filling missing player poses and returns no contacts for this rally. The rest of the video completes. The likely chain is **bad court → rejected or misselected players → pose interpolation failure**. The exact interpolation trigger has not been verified. Both systems load the same configured pose files; this is not evidence that the files were missing.

Our annotator also returns no contacts with supplied rally boundaries, using either court set. Full discovery separates the short camera cut and fits the normal views independently. It finds **21 of 24 labelled contacts**, all within two frames; **19 have the correct player**.
