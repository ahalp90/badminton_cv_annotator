These verdicts apply to the right-hand panels in marking_followup_visual_check.html.
They do not transfer to the previous fits, other candidates or acceptance decisions.

  The farther horizontals now overshoot instead of undershooting. No improvement

  away from the horizontals is marginally less bad. No new overall approval.

flawed. GX and Amateur-2 are rejected wrong-structure/wrong-identity controls.

pixel tolerance, new paint convention or lens-distortion model is established.
The current average-score ranking remains unsuitable as a general replacement.
