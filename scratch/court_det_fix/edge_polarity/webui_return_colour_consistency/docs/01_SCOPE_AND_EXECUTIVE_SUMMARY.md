# Scope and executive summary

## Audit question

Can the available court evidence distinguish Am1's false far baseline—the
bottom white tape of the net—from a legitimate partial court such as GX, and
what smallest additional check makes the distinction defensible?

The work was deliberately bounded:

- two frozen cases only;
- exact frozen parents, assignments and fitted point membership;
- no centre-label fitting repetition;
- no family-retention screen;
- no candidate search;
- no broader gallery evaluation;
- no production edit.

## Verified conclusion

The current evidence cannot make the semantic distinction. It measures line
geometry, greyscale ridge appearance, player containment and a restricted
camera model. None says whether a bright band is floor paint, net tape, a board
or another object.

Am1's strong-centre interpretation is not merely an optimiser failure. It is a
full-rank, finite, convex, positive-denominator fit that is semantically wrong.
The original-label and polarity-only fits do fail geometrically, but that does
not invalidate the separate strong-centre result.

GX proves that frame containment is not a valid remedy: one fitted GX corner is
below the working image, yet the visible court is a legitimate partial view.

## Meaningful finding

A local, one-sided **same-paint chroma veto** was tested after the images were
supplied. It keeps all geometry and membership fixed and asks whether fragments
claimed as the far baseline have the same local opponent-colour increment as
other transverse court markings.

| Result | Am1 | GX |
|---|---:|---:|
| Usable far-baseline fragments | 5 | 0 |
| Usable transverse references | 4 | 14 |
| Boundary median `(Δa, Δb)` | `(6.0, 0.0)` | not evaluated |
| Reference median `(Δa, Δb)` | `(12.75, 64.0)` | not evaluated |
| Colour separation | `64.35` | not evaluated |
| Separation / robust scale | `5.97` | not evaluated |
| Decision | reject paint mismatch | no rejection; unsupported/partial |

This is high-value because it tests **membership in the court's paint system**,
not merely the presence of a bright ridge. It is still exploratory: two examples
cannot calibrate a production threshold, and white court paint versus white net
tape may remain indistinguishable.

## Negative result preserved

The predeclared outward attached-mesh test predicted rejection on Am1 and no
contradiction on GX. It found one qualifying attachment in each case and rejected
neither. That result is preserved unchanged and the feature is not recommended.
