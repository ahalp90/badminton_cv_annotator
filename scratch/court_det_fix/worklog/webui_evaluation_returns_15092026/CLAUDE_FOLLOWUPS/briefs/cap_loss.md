## Why

The direction experiment found three view-arms where the best direction fit lies within 5 working pixels of the control while the pool of proposed courts holds nothing within 20 to 44 pixels: arm M on GX0 (fit 19.7, pool 33.8), arm R on Amateur-3 frame 0 (fit 4.3, pool 44.2), arm B on Amateur-2 frame 28019 (fit 3.0, pool 15.3). The matcher keeps at most 256 courts per direction pair before pooling, and nobody saved what it discarded. Record it.
