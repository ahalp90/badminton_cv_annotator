THE CAUSAL CHAIN, ASSEMBLED

At many rally ends the real shuttle becomes untrackable and the track
jumps into a fabricated loop. That jump often registers as the
rally's final contact. The fabricated bobbing reads as static under
the settle test. Under the old pool, "is the shuttle held at a
wrist" scanned EVERY court-scale detection, and someone's wrist was
usually near the fabricated mid-frame point, so the settle cap was
vetoed and stayed dormant; the search window ran on, and the loop's
own y-oscillation supplied a "descending run" whose terminal (the
fixed impossible point) became the landing and coin-flipped into
correctness. Under commit 11's sticky evidence, only the two tracked
players can say "held"; at these post-rally frames they cannot (the
tracker has legitimately reset at the scene cut, or the picks stand
far from the fabricated point), so the cap fires within 4-7 frames
of the fake contact and the landing honestly returns no answer.

The contact-precision fall is the same shape on the other side: the
pool-era divisor (a per-frame pool re-search) failed on many frames
and each failure silently blocked that contact at the wrist gate.
The sticky divisor is defined wherever the pick has a height in the
window, so 65/91/119 more contacts per fixture reach the fixed 1.4
threshold, and the extra passers are disproportionately junk. Both
regressions are the removal of accidental filters, not new defects.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE READING OF THE NUMBERS

The landing regression is now counted, not estimated. Within the
patched chain, pool kinematics scores 42/113 and sticky scores
22/113. Of that 20-count gap, 21 pool-correct calls sit on the 38
flipped rallies, and every one of them was an always-TOP guess
whose correctness equals the GT-happened-to-be-TOP tally. The
settle cap's documented purpose is suppressing exactly this
terminal class (the elevated post-rally carry that projects past
the far baseline). Commit 11 therefore does not lose discriminating
landing calls; it stops shipping a non-discriminating guess that
lucked into about 21 points.

The earlier "13 co-tuning residue" hypothesis is largely dissolved
by the lattice lock and the 6-of-13 always-TOP pattern: the 13
behave like the 25, differing only in the zone-length cut used by
the census. A settle/carry resweep may still be worth running after
landing, but it should not be expected to recover genuine calls
from the flipped set; there is no evidence any existed.

THE TWO OFF-SCREEN GUARDS (measured as a follow-up check,
2026-07-22)

window_end carries two designed guards, both untouched by the c11
patch (the patch edits only test fixtures around window_end):
sustained loss (an 8+ frame invisibility run at 25 fps closes the
window) and the top-exit exception (a loss run whose last visible
sample sat within 2% of the frame top keeps the window open for the
lob's re-entry). Pilot measurements over the 102 bisect windows:

- 61 of 102 windows contain a real (unfilled) sustained-loss run;
  the loss guard still fires on this substrate
- 16 of those follow a top-edge sample: the lob exception is primed
  and consulted on real footage
- BUT in 78 of 102 windows a fabricated fill run of 8+ frames
  appears BEFORE any real loss run. There the filler holds the
  track "visible" through what should have been the loss, and the
  loss guard is pre-empted. Post-c11 the settle cap closes these
  windows instead (fill reads static and not-held); pre-c11 nothing
  closed them, which is exactly where the fabricated landings lived
- the lob-loss check: 0 of the 64 sticky-None rallies contain a
  top re-entry descent after the sticky cap. On pilot, the early
  cap cost no genuine lob landings
- unmeasurable from this cache: windows where the filler destroyed
  the invisibility signal entirely; only a raw no-inpaint retrack
  (~22 min at 288p per the track README) could reconstruct the
  counterfactual
